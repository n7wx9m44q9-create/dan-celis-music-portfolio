Interactive game-music concept

Original music developed around gameplay and environment states. The concept uses thematic variation, instrumentation, filtering, and changes in intensity to distinguish exploration, tavern, night, and combat.

  • Instrumental variations designed to keep extended exploration engaging.
  • Battle music and a rise in intensity linked to combat and low health.
  • Return to the night theme after combat.
